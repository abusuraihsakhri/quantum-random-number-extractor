Interpretation and limitations

The von Neumann rule removes fixed bias only under independent, identically distributed Bernoulli assumptions. Correlations may remain. The displayed min-entropy statistic is a plug-in estimate based on observed symbol frequencies, not a conservative lower bound or a NIST SP 800-90B assessment.


These outputs do not establish quantum origin, physical entropy quality or cryptographic suitability. Never use them directly for key generation without validated entropy-source analysis and approved conditioning.
